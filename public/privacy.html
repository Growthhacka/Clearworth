Privacy notice

Plain-language summary of how Clearworth handles your data. If you run your own copy of Clearworth, the person or organisation running it is the data controller, and should replace this notice with their own details and legal contact.

What is stored

Your name, email address and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), your country and home currency, and the debts, receivables, investments, goals, payments and net worth history you choose to enter.

What it is used for

Only to show you your own portfolio. Your data is not sold, shared with advertisers or used to train models. Clearworth does not connect to your bank and never asks for card numbers or account numbers, so please do not enter them in notes fields.

Cookies

One strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in (HTTP-only, same-site). A theme preference is kept in your browser storage. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Exchange rates

The server may fetch public exchange rates. That request contains no information about you.

Your rights

You can export all of your data (JSON or CSV) and permanently delete your account and every record from Settings at any time.

Not financial advice

Clearworth is a tracking and calculation tool. Figures such as projected interest and debt-free dates are estimates based on what you enter. They are not financial, legal or tax advice.
